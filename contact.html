Frequently Asked Questions

How can I extract spatial datasets from this portal?

Navigate to the 'Geo-Portal' page, view the Beliatta maps, and click the blue 'Download Map' button in the extraction table.

Who developed this Geo-Platform?

This web portal was created by Chathumi, an Undergraduate student at the Department of Town & Country Planning, University of Moratuwa.

What are the data sources used for Beliatta?

Spatial data was gathered through regional field surveys, local administrative records, and spatial information mapping exercises.
